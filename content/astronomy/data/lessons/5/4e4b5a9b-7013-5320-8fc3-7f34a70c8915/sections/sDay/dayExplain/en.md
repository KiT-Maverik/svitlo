### A Difference Does Not Mean the Objects Disappeared
When it is dark, the tree and house are still in the same place; they are simply harder to see. A streetlight may shine at night, but it does not turn night into day.

**Sometimes you can see the Moon during the day, too.** So you cannot decide whether it is day or night just by looking for the Moon, stars, clouds, or artificial lights.

We have already noticed a change in the amount of light. Later we can ask another question: **why do day and night alternate?**
