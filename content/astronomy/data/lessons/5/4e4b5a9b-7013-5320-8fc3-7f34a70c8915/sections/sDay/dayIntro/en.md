## The Same Place, Different Lighting
These are **teaching diagrams**, not photographs. They show the same tree, house, hill, and the **sky above them**. In the first diagram, daylight illuminates the sky and landscape; in the second, the same place and sky look darker at night. First compare the diagrams, then compare two real photographs whose locations and observation times have been verified. Notice not only the objects but also the sky above them.

**Compare:** What changed? What stayed the same?

Noticing and naming a difference is **observing**. You do not need to explain why night comes yet. And never look directly at the Sun.
