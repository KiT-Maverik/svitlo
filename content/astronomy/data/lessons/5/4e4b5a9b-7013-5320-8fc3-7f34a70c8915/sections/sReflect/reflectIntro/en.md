## What Did We Notice?
**Day and night:** the sky over the same place can be lit by daylight or darker at night.

**The Moon:** on different days, we see different amounts of its lit disk.

These are **two different observations**. So we can ask two questions:
- “Why do day and night alternate?”
- “Why does the appearance of the Moon's lit part change?”

First **ask or select a question about day and night**, then **a separate question about the Moon's appearance**. For either topic, you can choose a complete ready-made question or **make up your own** — say it aloud or write it if you wish. If speaking or writing is difficult, choose the complete ready-made question on a card with a picture and audio. If you make up your own, ask the question first, **then confirm separately** that you have asked it. Just choosing “I want to make my own” does not mean you have asked a question. **You do not need to answer yet.** Writing is optional. You may also skip ahead.
