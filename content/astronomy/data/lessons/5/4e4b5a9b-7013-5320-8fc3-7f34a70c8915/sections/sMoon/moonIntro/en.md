## Look at the Moon on Different Days
Two **teaching diagrams** show the same round Moon. But **the lit part of its visible disk** looks different:
- **January 26, 2026** — first quarter: about half of the Moon's visible disk is lit.
- **February 1, 2026** — full Moon: almost the entire visible disk is lit.

These dates match verified astronomical data. The diagrams are **not photographs** taken from a particular location.

**What do you notice?** You do not need to explain yet why the visible lit part of the Moon changes.
