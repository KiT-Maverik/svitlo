## Now you know how to keep a sky journal

Say, show, or finish these three sentences:

- “I noticed…” / “The learning description says…”
- “This happened… I know because…”
- “One difference between the two records is…”

**Remember:** “Not mentioned” does not mean “not there.” A guess is not a fact.

When you have another **safe** observation or a captioned description, make another record and compare them.
