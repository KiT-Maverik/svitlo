## Example record

Here is a **made-up learning description**: “In the evening, the Moon and two clouds are visible in the sky. No other details are given.” This is not a photograph of the real sky or our own observation.

- **What:** The Moon and two clouds.
- **When:** Evening — that is what the description says.
- **Where or source:** The learning description “Evening Sky”; the place is not stated.
- **How:** We read the description.
- **Conditions:** Two clouds are mentioned; no other conditions are stated.

**A finished short record:** “The learning description ‘Evening Sky’ says that the Moon and two clouds were visible in the evening. The location is not stated.”

Another person can understand **what was reported and where the information came from**, without confusing a description with your own observation.
