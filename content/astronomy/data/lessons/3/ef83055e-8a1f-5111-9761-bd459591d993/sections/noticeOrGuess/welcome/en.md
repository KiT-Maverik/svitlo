# My First Sky Journal

Imagine a friend who did not see the sky you looked at. How could you help them understand **what you noticed**?

Today you will make a short record about the sky. You can use a few words, symbols, a drawing, or an explanation spoken aloud. You do not need long sentences.

If you have not observed the sky yourself, use a clearly identified learning description instead. You do not need to go outside at night, have a telescope, take photos, or give your address. If you look at the real sky, **never look directly at the Sun, including through optical instruments without special protection**.
