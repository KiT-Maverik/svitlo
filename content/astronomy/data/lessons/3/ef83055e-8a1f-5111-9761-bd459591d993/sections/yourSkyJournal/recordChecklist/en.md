## Make a record without the interactive activity

**Choose either your own safe observation or this complete account from a real source:** NASA's caption for the photograph “Supermoon in Washington” says that **on June 23, 2013, the Moon was rising behind the Washington Monument in Washington, USA**. The photographer was **Bill Ingalls, NASA**. NASA's caption does not state the exact time, weather, or cloud cover. This is **not your own observation**: you are reading or hearing information from a published photograph's caption. [Original source](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/supermoon-washington/). You do not need to open the website or see the photograph to complete this activity.

**Make your own short record** using the information above (do not copy the earlier finished example about made-up clouds). If you cannot read yet, ask someone to read this NASA description and all five questions aloud; **you must still create your own record**. Say your answers, draw pictures with labels, use symbols, or write them:

**What?** …  
**When?** …  
**Where or from what source?** …  
**How did I find this out?** …  
**What conditions are known?** …

If you use NASA's photograph, **name NASA/Bill Ingalls as your source and say that you read or heard the caption**, rather than claiming you saw this Moon yourself. Include the date June 23, 2013, and the city of Washington. **Do not make up** an exact time, cloud cover, or the photographer's precise position: if the caption does not say, write “not stated.”

**When is a record useful?** It should contain at least one specific detail (“The Moon was rising behind the Washington Monument”), an honest source or observation mode, and a way of obtaining the information. Include a time and conditions only as precisely as the source allows. If your record says only “not stated” and gives no specific detail or source, return to the description and add what is missing.

**Check your work:** Ask someone to tell you **what exactly** your record describes, **which source** it came from, and **how** you found it out. If information is missing, say so honestly. If the source or detail is unclear, improve your record. There are no points, and handwriting is not graded.
