# «Безпечний вибір»: Scene — editorial specification (not implemented)
Status: Scene configurator is authored, but the local Scene and its registry do not exist. The Stage 2/3 draft keeps config.params.disabled: true; the UI Kit ContentBlockRenderer skips disabled items before dispatching the Scene block. Content remains valid as a fully accessible text-first practiceReveal Checkpoint even if Scene is never mounted. No Scene playback or network-independent Scene code is claimed.

## Learning experience and invariant truth
Three situations in fixed order photo, glasses, clouds, each with a prompt, one safe and one risk action, and distinct safeFeedback/riskFeedback, sourced from this node's locale.value. All cases are unscored formative learning, not Question Bank data. Key safe is always the safe action. A mistake must lead only to explanatory feedback, never an instruction to perform an unsafe physical action or emulate a solar view.

## Explicit displayed option order
For photo render [risk, safe]; for glasses render [safe, risk]; for clouds render [risk, safe]. These stable per-case sequences are part of the design: do not derive them from alphabetical keys, implicit map iteration, side color or a rule that always puts safe first. Preserve logical keyboard/screen-reader order equal to the visible order and semantically stable option labels. The separate text-first Checkpoint uses the matching A/B order.

## Localized control copy
Read the exact Ukrainian keys from locale.value.controls: next, restart, textAlternative, feedbackHeading and progressPhoto/progressGlasses/progressClouds. Do not hardcode «Далі» or other visible/assistive copy inside React. Resolve all future declared languages from real reviewed translations only; no English fallback masquerading as a translation.

## States, actions and feedback
Initial state: intro, progressPhoto, first case prompt and two action buttons with neither selected. Activating either action by touch, Enter or Space reveals the matching safeFeedback/riskFeedback next to the buttons and announces it once politely for screen readers. The child can activate the other option to change their mind, with a fresh explanation and no penalty. After any response next appears; require no timer, score, grade or forced-success gate. Move to the next case, then done with restart available. Restart resets all local Scene selections to case one. Maintain predictable focus and allow learner navigation to the textAlternative pathway. Avoid duplication of visible Scene and complete text practice when both are enabled; the equivalent Checkpoint must always remain independently reachable.

## Accessibility and non-reader facilitation
Use native labelled buttons, no drag/drop, flashing light, color-only coding or animated staring at Sun. Announce progress and feedback accessibly, keep focus order visible and keyboard/touch-screen-reader parity, scale content 200%, honor reduced motion and RTL semantics. For a pre-reader: the facilitator reads the prompt and the two options aloud, accepts a spoken or pointing answer, reads the distinct feedback, and pauses so the child can choose again. Recorded narration/TTS is not implemented; the source text is the approved read-aloud script. No webcam, GPS, live Sun exposure, browser audio requirement or special equipment.

## Nonvisual and low-end route, crash guard
The text-first afterPractice + practiceReveal Checkpoint precedes this disabled Scene in section.order. It is the *primary* Draft exercise, not a fallback that appears only after a crash. Current UI Kit Scene.tsx throws on an unknown Scene ID and Suspense fallback=null does not catch that throw. At Stage 6 the Coding Agent must implement/register the real Scene and guard attempted mounting with a real error boundary or Scene-aware routing that renders the same independent text practice on missing/chunk-error/offline failure; only then may disabled be removed. Verify that children can always reach practiceReveal, including no script/network and slow devices, rather than relying on the next Markdown sibling to survive a render crash. No renderer/platform change is authored at this stage.

## Visual constraints and Stage 6 evidence
Use quiet 2D icons (photo, ordinary sunglasses, clouds), never simulated direct looking or sunlit bright-flash effects, DIY projector instructions, live camera or forbidden magnifiers. All graphics have explanatory text; preserve lesson neutrality. Scene acceptance requires real code+registry, keyboard/touch/screen reader, accessible live region, deterministic option order, all localized labels, explicit fallback, offline package/low-end verification and all three distinct feedback sets. Do not claim a CI-green YAML or successful text practice proves Scene completion.
