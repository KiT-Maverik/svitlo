# Skill Taxonomy Review

**Subject:** Biology  
**Original review date:** 2026-10-28  
**Additional editorial decisions:** 2026-10-29  
**Document type:** Dated decision history, not the canonical Skill catalog

This record preserves the editor's **specific decisions, reasons and changes of direction** made while shaping the Biology Skills Taxonomy on October 28–29, 2026. Earlier decisions are kept where later decisions superseded them, so the reasoning is not lost. It is **not** a live instruction list, a proposed implementation plan, or a substitute for the actual [Skills Taxonomy](../skills/taxonomy.md). The full original discussion log also remains in Git history.

### 2026-10-28 — Removal of general data, inquiry and evidence operations

The editor agreed that a generic operation does **not** belong in Biology simply because its example mentions organisms. The **context-substitution test** was used: if the same competence can be assessed with weather, finance or another subject instead of biological data, its primary ownership is not established by the Biology example.

**Four data/source-literacy candidates were removed from Biology:**

| Candidate                                                | Recorded reason                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Інтерпретувати таблицю росту організмів`                | Reading and comparing tabular values are general data operations.                                                     |
| `Розпізнавати біологічну закономірність на графіку`      | Recognizing a plotted trend is transferable graph literacy; explaining the _biological mechanism_ would be different. |
| `Порівнювати надійність джерел біологічної інформації`   | Evaluating credibility applies across subjects.                                                                       |
| `Виявляти біологічне твердження, не підтверджене даними` | Identifying unsupported claims is general scientific/critical reasoning.                                              |

The now-empty `Біологічні дані та наукові твердження` Group was removed.

**The `Дослідження та докази` Group was removed with six candidate Skills:** `Формулювати дослідницьке запитання`, `Робити перевірюване передбачення`, `Визначати змінну досліду`, `Контролювати умови порівняння`, `Інтерпретувати результати`, `Обґрунтовувати висновок даними`. These were judged generic scientific-method competencies rather than uniquely biological outcomes.

**Important boundary of this decision:** removing these candidates from Biology did **not** conclude that data literacy or the scientific method are unnecessary, nor did it assign new Skill IDs to another textbook. No cross-subject reassignment was decided on these dates.

### 2026-10-28 — Observation, classification and modeling

**Observation:** the initial decision was to move and rename `Виділяти спостережувані ознаки організму` to `Виділяти біологічно значущі ознаки організму` under Biological Classification. The other three candidates were removed:

- `Фіксувати зміни в часі`: generic recording of observations.
- `Використовувати збільшувальні засоби`: handling equipment is not automatically a Biology-specific Skill; a virtual Question cannot itself establish microscope proficiency.
- `Відділяти спостереження від припущення`: general scientific reasoning.

The Observation Group was removed. **This initial decision to retain the diagnostic-traits Skill was later superseded on 2026-10-29** (see below).

**Modeling:** the entire `Моделювання біологічних систем` Group was removed. Its four candidates were `Будувати модель зв’язку будови й функції`, `Прогнозувати наслідки зміни компонента біосистеми`, `Відрізняти модель від реального явища`, and `Порівнювати дві моделі одного явища`. Building or comparing visual models is not by itself an independent biological competence; the broadly transferable model-literacy operations did not warrant Biology-specific IDs.

Instead of adding a new ecology Skill, the existing `Моделювати взаємодію живих і неживих компонентів` was **renamed and redefined** as `Пояснювати взаємозалежність компонентів екосистеми`. The independent biological outcome is understanding connections among species, resources and nonliving factors, including indirect and cascading effects.

**Learning examples discussed:** intentional introduction of mongooses on Amami-Ōshima, accidental arrival of brown treesnakes on Guam, introduced rabbits and cats in Australia, the dodo's extinction on Mauritius, and post-contact epidemics among Indigenous peoples of the Americas. These are **six distinct case-study proposals**, not six new Skills. Full source links, causal qualifications and age-appropriate editorial cautions are preserved in [Studio Subject Proposition](../../../../studio/Subject%20Proposition.md) under **“Біологія: банк перевірених сюжетів для майбутніх уроків”**. The sixth story crosses Biology and History rather than serving as a simple ecological predator analogy.

### 2026-10-29 — What the Skill-versus-Question problem taught us

Five nominal Biology Skills had in fact described **ways to conduct or answer tasks**, not five distinct biological learning outcomes. For example, “sort mammals and reptiles into groups” can assess the established Skills identifying mammals or reptiles; a visible sorting action does not automatically justify a separate Biology Skill.

The same distinction accounted for two other removals: comparing already-taught selection and genetic modification, and predicting one narrow food-chain intervention when wider ecosystem interdependence was already represented.

**Agreed editorial principle:** a doubtful Skill/Question distinction must be raised with the editor **explicitly**, including a sample Question, existing underlying Skill(s), the claimed independent competency and alternative options. Neither silently adding nor silently deleting a disputed candidate is an editorial approval. The methodological rule is formally documented in [Create Skills Taxonomy](../../../../studio/skills/create-skills-taxonomy.skill.md), the Methodologist Persona and the [Skill entity contract](../../../../studio/docs/entities/skill.md).

This episode **did not** establish a blanket ban on classification, comparison, modeling or prediction Skills. They can be legitimate competencies when there is an independently useful disciplinary outcome.

## 2026-10-29 — Full-discipline exception review decisions

**Scope (APPROVED):** Biology is a complete discipline-wide taxonomy, without ceilings on age, curriculum level or methods. Specific Curricula decide selection, progression and instructional depth. Existing taxonomies elsewhere are not required to share identities with Biology Skills.

**New competencies (APPROVED):** Distinguish mitosis from meiosis; cover photosynthesis, cellular respiration and lung ventilation separately; distinguish transmission of genetic information, inheritance mechanisms and expression of genetic information; include evolutionary theory, common ancestry, speciation and multiple mechanisms of evolutionary change rather than treating natural selection as the entire theory. Add diagnostic competencies for major plant and invertebrate groups, bacteria versus archaea, microscopic eukaryotes, prokaryotic cell structure and bacterial division. Expand anatomy and physiology to digestion, absorption, kidneys, endocrine regulation, homeostasis, lung ventilation and human reproduction. Extend ecology to food webs, energy flow and matter cycling.

**Redundancy / granularity (APPROVED):** Remove generic plant sorting by visible traits: a classification Question can assess specific plant knowledge without an extra Skill. Remove “match animal to food source” as redundant with diet classification, and generic observation-description as an independent zoological Skill. Generalize the butterfly life-cycle Skill to complete insect metamorphosis. Retain both plant adaptation recognition and functional explanation with explicit descriptions distinguishing them. Two Skills in different Biology Domains may legitimately examine different disciplinary aspects of similar subject matter; they retain independent identities when the definitions and assessable outcomes are distinct.

**Regional context (APPROVED):** Retain recognition of locally common plants and animals: concrete instances differ with the learner's Program/region, but the competency identity does not. Retain the distinct Ukraine-specific natural-communities Skill for its intentional regional subject matter. Generalize Red Book species conservation beyond Ukraine; distinguish national/regional Red Books from the IUCN Red List.

**Accuracy and editorial wording (APPROVED):** Clarify the genotype/environment interaction in hereditary-versus-acquired traits; separate cell respiration from lung ventilation, and gas exchange from ventilation; drop model/“simple” limitations in Skill names; clarify biology-specific versus cross-domain ecological relationships. Keep the concise context-dependent microorganism-decomposition title and context-dependent plant light/air names unchanged. Remove taxonomy-level biomimicry references because engineering design belongs to Engineering. Strip the “Code:” prefix from all placeholder links to match the current canonical template. The revised taxonomy is not thereby fully approved.

**Synchronization requirement (APPROVED):** Reconcile the Biology branch against the current main-branch Subject Proposition containing 15 textbooks, including Philosophy and Psychology. Preserve earlier Biology subject-boundary decisions and prior Review entries while updating the current taxonomy.

## 2026-10-29 — Final editorial approval and faithful English translation

**APPROVED — final editor decision.** The editor explicitly approved the complete Biology taxonomy as a subject-wide catalog with **11 SkillDomains, 26 SkillGroups and 147 Skills** after resolving the preceding exception-review recommendations. The canonical [taxonomy](../skills/taxonomy.md) was translated into English in full, including all Domain, Group and Skill names, every substantive description, scope qualification and scientific caveat, without changing the underlying competency inventory or its primary hierarchy. The translated English document is the approved editorial source of truth.

**Implementation remains separate:** all **184** Domain/Group/Skill implementation links remain `[Not implemented](#)`. Final editorial approval is not a claim that UUIDs, Code files, Curricula, Lessons, Questions or learner assessment data exist. All historical decisions and earlier draft snapshots above remain preserved.

**Unresolved allocation as of 2026-10-29:** which other textbooks, if any, should own general data-literacy, research-method, observation, equipment-handling and cross-disciplinary modeling Skills was **not decided** in this review. Their removal from Biology is not the same as removing the competencies from education.
