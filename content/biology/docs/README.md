# Biology editorial documents

The [Biology Skills Taxonomy](skills/taxonomy.md) is the **editor-approved, English-language canonical taxonomy** (approved October 29, 2026), containing 11 Domains, 26 Groups and 147 Skills. Its substantive descriptions and classifications were translated from the agreed Ukrainian source without changing the approved competencies.

The dated [Skill Taxonomy Review](history/skill-taxonomy-review.md) preserves **editor-approved decisions, their reasoning and superseded proposals** from October 28–29, 2026. It is historical context, **not** a second canonical taxonomy or a list of pending instructions. The six source-backed teaching scenarios are maintained in [Studio Subject Proposition](../../../studio/Subject%20Proposition.md).

No per-entity Definition documents are expected: the taxonomy itself is the canonical pedagogical source. The taxonomy is editorially **approved**, but taxonomy code, UUIDs, curricula and lessons have **not** been created. Runtime implementation is a separate authorized stage.
