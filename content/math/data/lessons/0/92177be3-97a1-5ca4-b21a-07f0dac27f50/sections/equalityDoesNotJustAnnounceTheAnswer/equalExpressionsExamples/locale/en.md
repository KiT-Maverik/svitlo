# Pip and the Great Pond Parade

Pip the duck arrived with **five** shiny pebbles: three red and two blue. Gus the goose brought four green pebbles and one gold. His pile looked *much* grander.

> “I have more!” Gus honked, fluffing his feathers.

Pip tapped her beak. “Let's count before we crown you King of Pebbles.”

## The parade plan

1. Line up the pebbles on lily pads.
2. Count each pile:
   - Pip has `3 + 2`.
   - Gus has `4 + 1`.
3. Ask the smallest frog to judge.

```text
3 + 2 = 4 + 1
five = five
```

The frog sprang onto a drum. **“Same value!”** he squeaked. Gus stared at his “royal treasure” of a pile, then laughed so hard he fell into the pond.

“Equal doesn't mean identical,” Pip said, tossing him a ribbon. “You can wear it for *excellent splashing*.”

---

By sunset, Gus had a bow tie, Pip had a lily-leaf hat, and the frog had written the parade's new rule: **Count both sides.** Pip pinned it beside her [duck field guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duck), where even Gus could find it next time.
