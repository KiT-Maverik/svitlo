**%_%RED_NAME%_%** and **%_%BLUE_NAME%_%** are color names.

- Two circles can have different colors.
- A circle and a square can have the same color.
- A toy car can be red or blue. A cup can also be red or blue.

A color may look a little different on another screen. Focus on clearly red and clearly blue examples.

There are two color names here. $1 + 1 = 2$ is another way to count the names; you do not need to calculate anything or mix the colors.
