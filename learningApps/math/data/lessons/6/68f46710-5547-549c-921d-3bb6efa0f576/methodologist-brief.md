## Educational purpose

Move the start, not the finish: compare lengths from a shared zero. This Lesson is an autonomous introduction to its assigned canonical competency; it must be valuable without a Program storyline or assessments.

## Target audience and depth

**Primary grade: 1** (approximate ages 6–7). Early or mixed readers, multilingual learners, keyboard/switch users, low-vision users and learners who need concrete examples must receive equivalent access. **Instructional depth:** Direct endpoint alignment, equal cases, then indirect comparison with a transferable comparison strip. No ruler markings or metric conversions.

## Prerequisite knowledge and Skills

Understand longer/shorter in everyday speech; identify the two ends of a straight object; match objects by a common starting mark.

## Expected observable learning outcomes

- Compare two lengths by placing one endpoint of each at the same starting position.
- Identify the longer, shorter or equal length despite vertical offsets and different line thicknesses.
- Use one fixed reference strip to compare two objects which cannot be laid side by side.

## Essential mathematical ideas

Length is the distance between endpoints along a straight object or segment. For valid direct comparison, align one endpoint; indirect comparison requires the same unchanged reference unit or transferable measure.

## Conceptual progression

Start with a plausible misconception, invite a prediction, make the relevant invariant or comparison observable, provide controlled learner manipulation, explain counterexamples, and finish with a fresh independent application. Short formative responses are not proof of mastery.

## Common misconceptions and boundaries

Misleading displaced starts; thicker line mistaken for longer; counting visual pixels while ignoring alignment; using different reference strips.

Exclude unneeded symbolic formalism and unrelated geometry or measurement topics. Do not change any Skill definition to match these illustrations.
