# Lesson editorial source and role ownership

**Current authority:** [Create Lesson Flow](../../skills/lesson-workflow/create-lesson-flow.skill.md) owns a single temporary `lesson.yaml` through editorial Stages 1–5. This supersedes the former mandatory separate `methodology.md` and `content.yaml` authoring scheme **for new Lessons**. Historical files, independent reviews and source SHA must be deliberately migrated without silently erasing content or creating parallel canonical sources.

Methodologist owns root `production.methodology` and Stage 1 verified Skills, learner audience/prerequisites, scientific depth and sources, objectives, misconceptions and explicit Quiz `assessmentPlan`. For a Course-originated Lesson evaluate and reuse adequate already-prepared methodology; for standalone Lesson create the method from zero when absent and evaluate it when present. A Course relation must be verified, not inferred from methodology text. There is no artificial `Methodologist status: Approved` string or `draft/approved/stage` YAML frontmatter.

Apply the [Lesson methodology completeness contract](../entities/lesson.md#lesson-methodology-completeness--pedagogical-technical-specification) before the Screenwriter begins: per-Skill observable outcomes, actual scientific subject matter and examples, concept progression, likely misconceptions and justified assessment evidence must be specific enough for independent authoring. A generic heading list cannot be approved.

The Educational Screenwriter authors complete ordered Sections/ContentBlocks and actual source-language learner-facing explanations, examples, prompts/feedback and nested `production` specifications within the **same** YAML. Preserve the source `production.documentLanguage` and all intended `config.params.languages`, without invented translations. **Only Stage 3 Content Review requires a separate, independent new chat from the Stage 1/2 author.** The Stage 3 reviewer may document findings and directly fix in-scope content as a qualified editor, disclosing repair authorship. Stage 4 Experience and Stage 5 Methodology Compliance continue in **the same Stage 3 chat**, with explicit specialist-role changes and direct, role-authorized repairs instead of repeated Screenwriter handbacks. All audits record baseline/final YAML blob SHA, node-specific findings, correction commits and verdict in the **Lesson PR**, not YAML metadata. Any later correction materially affecting an earlier audit requires a scoped recheck against the final SHA in that same chat. Approved Methodologist scope and assessment-bank ownership remain protected.

At Resource Production, the completed YAML has one full structural check against Engine-derived `lesson.scaffold.schema.json` and manual production schema. The create-only Lesson Builder emits persistent typed modules, Markdown/Code, `methodology.md` and Scene/media specifications. Its entry must verify the current Stage 3 independent-review and Stage 4/5 specialist-audit evidence for the final YAML blob; reviewer corrections are not silently treated as independently re-reviewed by a different agent. Delete temporary `lesson.yaml` only after verified **complete and faithful** materialization including production instructions; a generated Draft is not automatically publishable. Questions/Flashcards maintain independently authorized YAML source, bank approvals and PRs. The Lesson remains Course-neutral.

Historical `lesson-content.schema.json` and `lesson-methodology-frontmatter.schema.json` remain readable for legacy migration only; they are not a new per-stage validation family.
