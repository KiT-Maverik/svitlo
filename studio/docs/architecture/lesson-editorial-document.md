# Lesson editorial source and role ownership

**Current Lesson authority:** [Create Lesson Flow](../../skills/lesson-workflow/create-lesson-flow.skill.md) and [Lesson workflow type contracts](../../../shared/engine/types/workflow/lessonWorkflow.types.ts). Stages 1–5 edit **one progressive TypeScript `draft.ts`**, not a temporary `lesson.yaml`. The Lesson Draft PR remains the sole stage and review tracker. All historical `lesson.yaml`, `methodology.md`, `methodologist-brief.md` and `content.yaml` documents and SHA-pinned reviews are migration evidence; do not destroy or silently overwrite them, and do not create a competing new authoring source.

## Stage 1 — Methodologist

`draft.ts` exports one object `satisfies LessonWorkflowStage1` with real immutable Lesson UUID, source title, verified Skill IDs, audience/completion intent in Engine-derived `config`, and substantive `production.methodology`. `production.languages.document` is the actual source locale and `production.languages.planned` is the nonempty future set; do not add runtime `config.params.languages` prematurely or fabricate target translations. Decide **all four independent** assessment booleans: `questionBankRequired`, `flashcardBankRequired`, `quizSectionRequired` and `flashcardsSectionRequired`. These do not replace the distinct `params.quizRequiredForCompletion` setting: if true, an **enabled** Quiz is mandatory in the materialized final Lesson. Justify Quiz intent, stable keys, Skill outcomes and cognitive `AssessmentMethodKey` operations without inventing actual Questions, selections or grading.

A Course-originated Lesson is handed off only as an initial, persisted UUID-addressed `draft.ts`, derived from Course Stage 3 new-Lesson methodology, objective/depth/sequence/assessment evidence and the Course's source/planned language policy. Stage 1 Methodologist verifies completeness/reuses content or repairs it in the same source; a Course handoff is neither Lesson approval nor permission to create Sections, banks or a Lesson PR. A standalone Lesson authors the same type independently. The [methodology completeness criteria](../entities/lesson.md#lesson-methodology-completeness--pedagogical-technical-specification) remain binding: objectives, prerequisites, scientific facts, examples, misconception boundaries, assessment evidence, accessibility/offline and sources must be substantive, not just type-correct.

## Stage 2 — Screenwriter

Expand the **same source** to `satisfies LessonWorkflowStage2`, adding ordered `config.section.items/order` and actual localizable explanations, examples, useful feedback, practice and accessible learning alternatives. Each nested Section/ContentBlock/Scene/Quest is a plain `{config,production?}` object with real Engine Config-derived fields; child Configurators are constructed **later**, in the authorized materializer. Scenes/media require complete implementable specifications and weak-device/offline equivalents. Quest has a Build-time schema but no registered runtime Lesson Section, so it remains a publishing blocker until supported. A Quiz/Flashcards Section may have partial Config parameters before real banks are authored: never invent Question/Flashcard UUIDs or a successful grading policy.

## Stages 3–5 — independent entry and role-owned direct corrections

Stage 3 Content Review starts in **a distinct chat/context from Stages 1–2** and examines the actual Stage 2 source plus evidence at a pinned baseline SHA. This independent qualified reviewer can directly correct in-scope content in the **same Lesson `draft.ts` and PR** under the appropriate role, then records correction SHA and own final verdict; a correction is not an additional independent signature. Stage 4 Experience Review and Stage 5 final Methodology Compliance continue in the **same Stage 3 chat** with explicit specialist-role changes. Each audit reads the current full Lesson, corrects approved-scope defects directly, reviews the effect of its own edits and records baseline/final source blob SHA and node findings in the PR.

Do not create `LessonWorkflowStage3`, `LessonWorkflowStage4`, `LessonWorkflowStage5`, a duplicate review source or a second translation/review PR. Earlier completed reviews keep historical SHAs; Stage 5's final integrated verdict covers the final corrected object. Material curriculum/assessment scope changes require the actual owner, but resume at Stage 5 once resolved — **never** cycle reviews backwards. The final object must `satisfies LessonWorkflowMaterializerInput` (alias of Stage 2), yet compilation is no substitute for substantive source and role-specific approval.

## Stage 6 and independent later production

Stage 6.1 invokes the **integrated trusted typed** Lesson/Section/ContentBlock materializers only after the workflow is deployed to default `develop` and source/review authorization is established; source-blob/HEAD pinning, preflight, no-clobber, immutable UUIDs and nested imports remain mandatory. Derived human-readable `methodology.md` is an **output**, not another editable methodology source. Incomplete Quiz/Flashcards sections may be explicit nonpublishable blockers pending reviewed bank resources; Quiz completion must preserve the enabled-Quiz invariant. Stage 6.2 completes/strictly checks actual modular Lesson resources and accessibility/offline paths; Stage 6.3 implements, registers and tests real React Scenes.

Parent Stage 7 Questions and Stage 8 Flashcards track independently authorized, seven-stage bank child workflows, initially handed off only as bank-owned **typed Stage 1 `draft.ts`**, not full bank materialization, invented bank members or an embedded Lesson bank. Translation and independent review at Stages 9/10 work **on real materialized Lesson source in the same Lesson PR**; no extra translator/reviewer PR, status ledger or automatic merge. Materialization atomically consumes the reviewed draft and creates the canonical Configurator `index.ts`, with exact Git commit/blob provenance. The historical 109 Math Lesson YAML files remain unchanged provenance, not an executable authoring pipeline.
