# Lesson

> New Lesson authoring uses one progressively typed `draft.ts`: Stage 1 `LessonWorkflowStage1`, Stage 2 `LessonWorkflowStage2`, with Stages 3–5 reviewing the same source. Source materialization is Stage 6.1 (after trusted typed orchestration is integrated), Stage 6.2 finishes real typed resources and Stage 6.3 implements/tests Scenes; Stages 7/8 independently author Questions/Flashcards, and 9/10 translate/review the Lesson in its existing Draft PR. Historical YAML must be migrated without loss, never silently deleted.

**Entity category:** ordinary Lesson with Sections.

**Lesson override identity:** `defineLesson.build.overrides(patch, { languages })` returns `{ id, base, locale }`, with `id` equal to the original Lesson UUID. The top-level `id` identifies a Lesson for Course planning; it is **not** an identity patch and is never merged into sparse Props. Direct Course reuse needs only the existing Lesson UUID; Course-specific adaptation can reference the same Lesson by the validated override result.

**Optional target audience in Engine Config:** `params.age` and `params.grade` use the same range contracts as Course (`{ min, max }`, age 0–125, grade 0–40, `min <= max`). They describe intended learners; neither proves individual learner eligibility nor changes Skills, Course ownership, or Lesson independence. Instructional Lessons are Course-neutral and reusable; a deliberately placed **assessment-only Lesson** may depend pedagogically on preceding Course Lessons and their Question Banks without a different Engine entity type.

An **instructional Lesson** independently teaches **one or more approved Skills** and must make sense without a Course, Curriculum, mascot, quest, reward system, external plot or special learner history. An **assessment-only Lesson** can instead measure approved Skills from earlier Course Lessons; its methodology may be Course-placement-dependent and should **not** pretend to teach or be independently reusable without its prerequisites. The Lesson owns its ordered instructional or assessment Sections and their content; a Course places it into a larger journey without duplicating that source.

## PEDAGOGICAL RESPONSIBILITIES

- Instructional Lessons present accurate content through appropriate explanations, demonstrations, interactions, guided practice and reflection; assessment-only Lessons elicit evidence for already taught Skills. No fixed Section count, fixed `theory → practice → quiz` recipe or one-Skill-per-Lesson rule.
- Link each Lesson's intended learning to the Methodologist's approved outcomes and Skill definitions; teaching, completing a Lesson, marking a checkpoint and demonstrating mastery are **different** concepts.
- A Lesson can have an **instructional sequence** and staged revelation. It has **no Course-owned narrative** (characters, missions, external story, points/rewards or quest progression). See [Course](./course.md) for Course-owned narrative and ordering.
- Lesson content is assembled from ordered [Sections](./section.md) with typed resources. Its educational design is authored by the Screenwriter in the Lesson's single evolving typed `draft.ts` `production`/`config` tree; the separate [Lesson Content](./lesson-content.md) document is historical migration context.

## Lesson methodology completeness — pedagogical technical specification

Stage 1 of [Create Lesson Flow](../../skills/lesson-workflow/create-lesson-flow.skill.md) must yield an **implementation-ready pedagogical specification** in the typed Stage 1 `draft.ts` `production.methodology`, not a short lesson synopsis. It defines **what, why, for whom, prerequisites, depth and evidence**; the Screenwriter and engineers decide **how** to realize the approved intent. New production does **not** require a parallel `methodology.md`, `content.yaml`, status frontmatter or production Issue. The Stage 6 materializer may publish durable methodology from verified source; preserve legacy material and its provenance when migrating.

For every target Lesson and Skill, the Methodologist specifies:

1. **Learners, scope and purpose:** for instruction, an independent Course-neutral educational need; for an approved assessment-only Lesson, the actual Course sequence/boundary and upstream Lesson slots/bank owners. State learner entry profile, literacy/language assumptions, approved canonical Skill UUIDs/source references, explicit boundaries and accessibility constraints.
2. **Observable outcomes and depth:** what a learner can actually demonstrate for **each** Skill, at what depth, distinguishing exposure, supported practice and independently assessable competence. A list of Skill names is insufficient.
3. **Prerequisites and conceptual sequencing:** prior knowledge, important terms, ordering/dependency rationale and transfer opportunities without hard-coding Course storyline or prescribed Section UI.
4. **Substantive subject matter:** accurate essential facts, definitions, relationships, mechanisms and important edge cases; suitable worked examples, counterexamples and misconception corrections when necessary. Never make the Screenwriter invent curriculum facts or scientific claims.
5. **Assessment obligations:** justified zero/one/multiple Quiz Section intents (not implied by owning a Question Bank), verified target Skills, appropriate `AssessmentMethodKey` cognitive operations and the required evidence. A substantive instructional Lesson normally has an independently authored full Question Bank **even when it has no Quiz Section**; exceptions require a concrete Methodologist rationale. An approved assessment-only Lesson may have **no own bank** and use explicit Question UUIDs from reviewed, published banks of earlier Lessons. During Course Stage 3/4 planning, identify upstream slots/Skills/owners without fabricating UUIDs; actual Quiz selection waits for integrated banks. Question writing, correct answers, retries, scoring and UI presentation belong to specialist workflows.
6. **Adaptation, sources and open decisions:** legitimate localization/cultural and accessible equivalents, offline/safety constraints, sources for contested claims, unresolved assumptions with a named owner and handoff blockers.

**Review gate:** could an independent Screenwriter prepare the complete Lesson source and resource specifications without guessing objectives, scientific content, depth, progression, misconceptions or assessment obligations? If not, Stage 1 is blocked and Stage 5 Methodology Compliance cannot approve it. No artificial token quota: specify enough for the actual subject, no more. Only the Methodologist/editor may substantively amend the approved scope; changed evidence invalidates affected SHA-specific reviews.

## EDITORIAL ARTIFACTS AND IMPLEMENTATION

**New work:** one Lesson UUID-addressed `draft.ts` containing `{ config, production }` is the single editable source. Stage 1 uses `satisfies LessonWorkflowStage1`, Stage 2 extends it to `satisfies LessonWorkflowStage2`, and Stages 3–5 review/correct exactly that Stage 2 object. The final type `LessonWorkflowMaterializerInput` aliases Stage 2. The source materializer derives readable `methodology.md`, Section and Block source, and final `defineLesson` modules only after Stage 6 authorization and actual integration. Historic files listed below are **migration provenance**, not a request to create new parallel `methodology.md` or `content.yaml`. Questions/Flashcards are independently authored Lesson-owned banks; an assessment-only Lesson may reference Questions owned by _other_ Lessons, without duplicating their bank source.

Reusable/standalone Lesson files live under `<PackageRoot>/data/lessons/<last-hex-digit>/<uuid>/`. Course-originated Lessons retain separate identity; instructional Lessons remain Course-neutral, but assessment-only Lessons may have reviewed Course-specific prerequisites; see [Episode](./episode.md) for Course-owned narrative activities. The [shared UUID addressing rule](../architecture/entity-uuid-addressing.md) determines this path from the permanent Lesson ID. Keep the human-readable camelCase slug in Lesson metadata, not in the directory name. Existing folders do not move when a slug changes.

- **Historical** `methodology.md` (or `methodologist-brief.md`): approved _what/why/for whom_, prerequisite, Skill scope and the Methodologist's assessment intent (no Quiz, or one/more Quizzes with appropriate Assessment Methods); an editorial-only source **not included in runtime configuration or client bundles**. Its `Target Skills` contains one direct relative Markdown link per Skill **to the canonical Skill `*.data.ts` path under `data/skills/`**, with the full source filename (including extension) as the link label. New Briefs **target `<PackageRoot>/data/skills/`** for Skill `*.data.ts` references. Validate every link to an existing source file. If that package has not migrated, report the gap as a blocker rather than writing broken links or pointing to `src/`.
- **Historical** `content.yaml`: Screenwriter-owned **machine-readable** _how to teach_ plan with ordered Sections/ContentBlocks, learner-facing text in the declared `documentLanguage` and explicit assessment/Scene/media dependencies. Legacy `content.md` requires explicit migration; it is not a parallel canonical artifact.
- `scenes/`: separate Scene specifications **only when needed**, later consumed by the Scene implementation workflow.
- **Historical assessment-bank YAML:** old `questions.yaml` and `flashcards.yaml` contain reviewable legacy provenance, **not** currently supported authoring sources. For new work the Assessment Architect authors `questions/draft.ts` (`QuestionBankWorkflowStage1 → Stage2 → Stage3`), followed by independently reviewed typed Stage 5 source materialization; the Flashcard Architect similarly authors `flashcards/draft.ts`. Both banks retain their own PRs and mandatory Question-first (or verified N/A) handoff. Flashcard flags stay a typed unordered `parameters.flags: []` array; relationship edges are authored once and resolved to stable Card UUIDs. See [assessment separation](../architecture/lesson-assessment-bank-separation.md).
- Typed `*.data.ts` configuration and published resources are owned by implementation/build workflows, not by either editorial document.

**Independent-bank contract:** [Engine Lesson Config and Props](../../../shared/engine/configurators/lessons/defineLesson/defineLesson.schemas.ts) contain stable Lesson identity, supported languages and ordered Sections, **not** embedded Questions or Flashcards. Quiz and Flashcards Sections refer to separately published resources by explicit UUID; a textbook-scoped manifest records `ownerLessonId` provenance and available languages. Folder addressing follows permanent UUIDs, not slugs. Global UUID assessment publication is implemented; full educational review, required translations, assets and release checks remain separate readiness gates.

Related: [historical Lesson Content](./lesson-content.md), [Section](./section.md), [Course](./course.md), [Skill](./skill.md).

## Typed authoring and publication boundary

Use [`defineLesson`](../../../shared/engine/configurators/lessons/defineLesson/defineLesson.ts) from `@svitlo/engine/build`. It composes the named, ordered `section` collection and declares `params.languages`; it does **not** own either bank. The `defineQuizSection` selection requires explicit `questionIds` that may be owned by **different source Lessons**; the `defineFlashcardsSection` selection requires explicit `flashcardIds`. Section screen ContentBlocks never define Questions. A Quiz selecting Questions from another Lesson must pass the implemented global UUID and locale resolver for the target textbook and locales before publication. Build-only independent Question and Flashcard configurators produce separately addressable Props, and the host supplies a selected-resource reader to UI Kit. Complete-release referential validation, source/review authorization, real media and full locales remain independent production gates.

`lesson.build.props({ languages })` produces `base` and sparse `locale` Props fragments. `lesson.build.overrides(patch, { languages })` prepares Course-specific patches without mutating the Lesson. Generators, not configurators, write files.

At runtime, [`assembleLessonProps`](../../../shared/engine/assemblers/assembleLessonProps/assembleLessonProps.ts) merges the published base and selected locale with optional Course overrides and validates the complete Props.

**Editorial localization:** for new Lessons, `production.languages.document` is the actual source language and `production.languages.planned` is the nonempty intended publication plan. Stage 1–5 intentionally omit `config.params.languages`; final source materialization derives the Engine language tuple without fabricating translations. Course consumers must request an actual supported language subset. Historical `content.yaml`/`lesson.yaml` inputs require non-destructive migration; real translated locale modules arrive in Stage 9 and independent review in Stage 10.

## Staged typed Lesson authoring and materialization

The canonical source is `content/<book>/data/lessons/<bucket>/<uuid>/draft.ts`. Its Stage 1 `config` reuses genuine `Lesson_Config` identity, Skills, source-language title and appropriate audience/completion parameters; `production` preserves the substantive Methodologist methodology, actual document language and planned languages, plus **four independent** `LessonProductionAssessmentRequirements` choices: own Question Bank, own Flashcard Bank, Quiz Section and Flashcards Section. These are not runtime Config parameters. `params.quizRequiredForCompletion` is a separate runtime rule: when true, final Lesson materialization must include an **enabled** Quiz Section, not a disabled or fake placeholder. Course Stage 5 creates the exact Stage 1 `draft.ts` for a new Lesson, retaining reviewed Course Stage 3 methodology and a persisted UUID — no Sections, banks, `defineLesson` or automatic Lesson PR.

Stage 2 adds ordered, slug-keyed `config.section.items/order`, with **plain** nested `{config,production?}` Section/ContentBlock nodes and actual source-language copy. Nested Carousel slides, Checkpoint items/answers and Quest stages/tasks are also plain typed authoring objects, not Configurator instances. Quiz/Flashcards Section parameter choices may remain partial until independently reviewed banks exist; never invent Question/Flashcard UUIDs, scoring, translation or implementation. Every Scene/media dependency requires implementable production specifications, accessibility and offline alternatives. Quest has a Build configurator but **no Lesson runtime Section registration**; keep its plan visibly nonpublishable until supported.

Editorial Stages 3–5 correct that same Stage 2 source and record source SHAs, reviewer roles and verdicts in the **existing Lesson PR**, not a new Stage 3/4/5 authoring type. Stage 6.1 consumes only the reviewed `LessonWorkflowMaterializerInput` after a trusted typed orchestrator and concrete materializers are available. Stage 6.2 validates and completes real resource modules; Stage 6.3 implements/registers/tests all Scenes (or records verified N/A). Stage 7/8 bank workflows each have separately authorized minimal Stage 1 `draft.ts` handoffs, not auto-generated bank members. Real translations follow materialization in the same Lesson PR at Stages 9/10.

**Migration boundary:** the old YAML Builder/Finalizer and Bank YAML builders/commands are removed. The trusted typed Lesson Stage 6.1 command is `/lesson materialize typed`, limited to valid pinned Stage 2 source without editorial or Course-provenance gates and available only after default-branch deployment. Historical YAML remains immutable provenance, not a live fallback or parallel editable source. The current [typed production architecture](../architecture/typed-production-materialization.md) governs source handoffs; publication and downstream banks/locales have distinct gates.

## Quest in standalone Lessons (planned runtime)

The [Quest Section](./quest.md) is designed for independently useful, accessible interactive demonstration and guided practice (e.g. manipulating a quadratic equation) without requiring characters or Course plot. It is not a Lesson subtype or a Quiz replacement. Quest Build contracts exist, but Lesson Section runtime registration and player integration are deferred. Do not publish a Quest as if supported; retain the approved Methodologist assessment and Skill boundaries.

### Independently authored Flashcards and Questions

The completed `defineFlashcard` Configurator retains typed flags, relationships and three structural sides; the front, back and optional hint use `defineFlashcardSide({ items, order })`. A side is _not_ independently identified and must not receive an invented UUID. The separate `flashcards/index.ts` TypeScript bank (when present) is compiled into individual UUID-addressed JSON objects, not a `Lesson.flashcards` collection. The Section's explicit `flashcardIds` determines order.

The independent Question and Flashcard Banks use their own reviewed, progressively typed `questions/draft.ts` and `flashcards/draft.ts` Stage 1–3 editorial sources. Their Stage 5 trusted materializers create complete source registries from exact reviewed SHA-pinned input; `generateAssessmentResources` publishes only completed Configurators. Historical YAML conversion and alternate draft builders are not supported.

**Global UUID publication:** Authored Question and Flashcard bank sources remain Lesson-owned. The published data lives under `data/questions/<last-uuid-hex>/<uuid>/` and `data/flashcards/<last-uuid-hex>/<uuid>/` with a shared `data/assessments/manifest.json`. The manifest's `ownerLessonId` is provenance rather than a cross-Lesson Quiz selection restriction. Complete production validation and CI are required before release.

## Assessment Section decisions and Lesson completion gate

The Stage 1 typed `production.assessment` requires **four independent booleans**: `questionBankRequired`, `flashcardBankRequired`, `quizSectionRequired` and `flashcardsSectionRequired`. Methodologist methodology must explain each choice and any additional Quiz or Flashcards Sections, including distinct target Skill evidence and cognitive operations. A bank is not the same as a corresponding Section; a Lesson can need its own Question Bank without a Quiz, and a Quiz can reference reviewed upstream Questions. Never make up bank members or assume missing requirements are `false`.

`config.params.quizRequiredForCompletion` remains a **separate** authoring/runtime policy, not the same as the four `production.assessment` booleans. If true, the final Lesson must contain an **enabled Quiz Section**; disabling a pending Quiz cannot satisfy this invariant. Actual scoring, Question selection and review are owned by the Assessment Architect. A provisional **already-authored, enabled** Section may remain parameter-incomplete in editorial Stages 2–5; its concrete Section renderer may produce visibly blocked source drafts at Stage 6.1. However, `materializeLesson` **rejects a Lesson missing an enabled required Quiz/Flashcards Section before source generation** rather than producing an invalid `defineLesson` with a TODO. A draft with unresolved selections is not release-ready: real reviewed bank UUIDs, complete Section Config parameters, required locales and successful Build/Props verification are still mandatory. `assertLessonSourceReleaseReady` catches materializer diagnostics and reserved assessment UUIDs, but its use in the parent publication pipeline and real bank-ownership validation remain integration obligations.

Staged typed authoring and materialization do not rely on a second `questions.yaml` or `flashcards.yaml` `sectionDrafts` document. Independent banks begin with separately authorized typed Stage 1 `draft.ts` handoffs and progress to independently reviewed Stage 3 before trusted Bank Stage 5. The retired unqualified `/lesson materialize` and `/lesson finalize` commands must not be used; the only Lesson handoff is `/lesson materialize typed`, available after default-branch rollout.
