# Lesson

> New authoring follows [Create Lesson Flow](../../skills/lesson-workflow/create-lesson-flow.skill.md): one temporary `lesson.yaml` through editorial review, Draft Lesson PR for workflow status, then Stage 6.1 source materialization, 6.2 completion/strict typing of generated resources and 6.3 executable Scene implementation/registry/tests, followed by parent Stages 7/8 for independently authored Questions/Flashcards child PRs. Historical separate `content.yaml`/`methodology.md` plans must be migrated without loss, not kept as duplicate new sources.

**Entity category:** ordinary Lesson with Sections.

**Lesson override identity:** `defineLesson.build.overrides(patch, { languages })` returns `{ id, base, locale }`, with `id` equal to the original Lesson UUID. The top-level `id` identifies a Lesson for Course planning; it is **not** an identity patch and is never merged into sparse Props. Direct Course reuse needs only the existing Lesson UUID; Course-specific adaptation can reference the same Lesson by the validated override result.

**Optional target audience in Engine Config:** `params.age` and `params.grade` use the same range contracts as Course (`{ min, max }`, age 0–125, grade 0–40, `min <= max`). They describe intended learners; neither proves individual learner eligibility nor changes Skills, Course ownership, or Lesson independence. Instructional Lessons are Course-neutral and reusable; a deliberately placed **assessment-only Lesson** may depend pedagogically on preceding Course Lessons and their Question Banks without a different Engine entity type.

An **instructional Lesson** independently teaches **one or more approved Skills** and must make sense without a Course, Curriculum, mascot, quest, reward system, external plot or special learner history. An **assessment-only Lesson** can instead measure approved Skills from earlier Course Lessons; its methodology may be Course-placement-dependent and should **not** pretend to teach or be independently reusable without its prerequisites. The Lesson owns its ordered instructional or assessment Sections and their content; a Course places it into a larger journey without duplicating that source.

## PEDAGOGICAL RESPONSIBILITIES

- Instructional Lessons present accurate content through appropriate explanations, demonstrations, interactions, guided practice and reflection; assessment-only Lessons elicit evidence for already taught Skills. No fixed Section count, fixed `theory → practice → quiz` recipe or one-Skill-per-Lesson rule.
- Link each Lesson's intended learning to the Methodologist's approved outcomes and Skill definitions; teaching, completing a Lesson, marking a checkpoint and demonstrating mastery are **different** concepts.
- A Lesson can have an **instructional sequence** and staged revelation. It has **no Course-owned narrative** (characters, missions, external story, points/rewards or quest progression). See [Course](./course.md) for Course-owned narrative and ordering.
- Lesson content is assembled from ordered [Sections](./section.md) with typed resources. Its educational design is authored by the Screenwriter in the Lesson's single `lesson.yaml` `production`/`config` tree; the separate [Lesson Content](./lesson-content.md) document is historical migration context.

## Lesson methodology completeness — pedagogical technical specification

Stage 1 of [Create Lesson Flow](../../skills/lesson-workflow/create-lesson-flow.skill.md) must yield an **implementation-ready pedagogical specification** in `lesson.yaml` `production.methodology`, not a short lesson synopsis. It defines **what, why, for whom, prerequisites, depth and evidence**; the Screenwriter and engineers decide **how** to realize the approved intent. New production does **not** require a parallel `methodology.md`, `content.yaml`, status frontmatter or production Issue. The Stage 6 materializer may publish durable methodology from verified source; preserve legacy material and its provenance when migrating.

For every target Lesson and Skill, the Methodologist specifies:

1. **Learners, scope and purpose:** for instruction, an independent Course-neutral educational need; for an approved assessment-only Lesson, the actual Course sequence/boundary and upstream Lesson slots/bank owners. State learner entry profile, literacy/language assumptions, approved canonical Skill UUIDs/source references, explicit boundaries and accessibility constraints.
2. **Observable outcomes and depth:** what a learner can actually demonstrate for **each** Skill, at what depth, distinguishing exposure, supported practice and independently assessable competence. A list of Skill names is insufficient.
3. **Prerequisites and conceptual sequencing:** prior knowledge, important terms, ordering/dependency rationale and transfer opportunities without hard-coding Course storyline or prescribed Section UI.
4. **Substantive subject matter:** accurate essential facts, definitions, relationships, mechanisms and important edge cases; suitable worked examples, counterexamples and misconception corrections when necessary. Never make the Screenwriter invent curriculum facts or scientific claims.
5. **Assessment obligations:** justified zero/one/multiple Quiz Section intents (not implied by owning a Question Bank), stable keys, verified target Skills, appropriate `AssessmentMethodKey` cognitive operations and the required evidence. A substantive instructional Lesson normally has an independently authored full Question Bank **even when it has no Quiz Section**; exceptions require a concrete Methodologist rationale. An approved assessment-only Lesson may have **no own bank** and use explicit Question UUIDs from reviewed, published banks of earlier Lessons. During Course Stage 3/4 planning, identify upstream slots/Skills/owners without fabricating UUIDs; actual Quiz selection waits for integrated banks. Question writing, correct answers, retries, scoring and UI presentation belong to specialist workflows.
6. **Adaptation, sources and open decisions:** legitimate localization/cultural and accessible equivalents, offline/safety constraints, sources for contested claims, unresolved assumptions with a named owner and handoff blockers.

**Review gate:** could an independent Screenwriter prepare the complete Lesson source and resource specifications without guessing objectives, scientific content, depth, progression, misconceptions or assessment obligations? If not, Stage 1 is blocked and Stage 5 Methodology Compliance cannot approve it. No artificial token quota: specify enough for the actual subject, no more. Only the Methodologist/editor may substantively amend the approved scope; changed evidence invalidates affected SHA-specific reviews.

## EDITORIAL ARTIFACTS AND IMPLEMENTATION

**New work:** the single temporary `lesson.yaml` with `config` and authoring-only `production` holds reviewed Methodologist and Screenwriter material through Stages 1–5 and is safely materialized in Stage 6. Historic files listed below are **migration provenance**, not a request to create new parallel `methodology.md` or `content.yaml`. Questions/Flashcards are independently authored Lesson-owned banks; an assessment-only Lesson may reference Questions owned by _other_ Lessons, without duplicating their bank source.

Reusable/standalone Lesson files live under `<PackageRoot>/data/lessons/<last-hex-digit>/<uuid>/`. Course-originated Lessons retain separate identity; instructional Lessons remain Course-neutral, but assessment-only Lessons may have reviewed Course-specific prerequisites; see [Episode](./episode.md) for Course-owned narrative activities. The [shared UUID addressing rule](../architecture/entity-uuid-addressing.md) determines this path from the permanent Lesson ID. Keep the human-readable camelCase slug in Lesson metadata, not in the directory name. Existing folders do not move when a slug changes.

- **Historical** `methodology.md` (or `methodologist-brief.md`): approved _what/why/for whom_, prerequisite, Skill scope and the Methodologist's explicit `assessmentPlan` (no Quiz, or one/more Quizzes with required `AssessmentMethodKey` values); an editorial-only source **not included in runtime configuration or client bundles**. Its `Target Skills` contains one direct relative Markdown link per Skill **to the canonical Skill `*.data.ts` path under `data/skills/`**, with the full source filename (including extension) as the link label. New Briefs **target `<PackageRoot>/data/skills/`** for Skill `*.data.ts` references. Validate every link to an existing source file. If that package has not migrated, report the gap as a blocker rather than writing broken links or pointing to `src/`.
- **Historical** `content.yaml`: Screenwriter-owned **machine-readable** _how to teach_ plan with ordered Sections/ContentBlocks, learner-facing text in the declared `documentLanguage` and explicit assessment/Scene/media dependencies. Legacy `content.md` requires explicit migration; it is not a parallel canonical artifact.
- `scenes/`: separate Scene specifications **only when needed**, later consumed by the Scene implementation workflow.
- `questions.yaml`: Assessment Architect's approved source-language Question bank (specialist workflow, subject to separate editorial approval). It is the mandatory upstream input for Flashcard authoring; the current Questions Builder creates typed drafts after independent approval.
- `flashcards.yaml`: Flashcard Learning Architect's source-language recall bank, written **only after** approved Questions. Includes typed ContentBlocks, **Flashcard-owned** `list`/`warning`/`acronym`/`custom` flags and one canonical bank-level relationship graph. The Engine Config, Flashcard YAML and published Props all use `parameters.flags: []` (an unordered typed array, without `items/order`). The current Builder rejects unsupported Question-field pseudo-references; record intended typed imports in the child PR instead of copying localized facts. The Engine materializes approved YAML edges on both cards using the relationship model in [Flashcard schemas](../../../shared/engine/configurators/flashcards/defineFlashcard/defineFlashcard.schemas.ts), with stable partner IDs. The existing UI Kit Flashcard now renders typed flags automatically. Locale-specific acronym wording still requires independent translation; UI relationship navigation and scheduler semantics remain separate capabilities.
- Typed `*.data.ts` configuration and published resources are owned by implementation/build workflows, not by either editorial document.

**Implemented independent-bank contract (PR #606 merged):** [Engine Lesson Config and Props](../../../shared/engine/configurators/lessons/defineLesson/defineLesson.schemas.ts) contain stable Lesson identity, supported languages and ordered Sections, **not** embedded Questions or Flashcards. Quiz and Flashcards Sections refer to separately published resources by explicit UUID; a textbook-scoped manifest records `ownerLessonId` provenance and available languages. Folder addressing follows permanent UUIDs, not slugs. Global UUID assessment publication is implemented; full educational review, required translations, assets and release checks remain separate readiness gates.

## Typed authoring and publication boundary

Use [`defineLesson`](../../../shared/engine/configurators/lessons/defineLesson/defineLesson.ts) from `@svitlo/engine/build`. It composes the named, ordered `section` collection and declares `params.languages`; it does **not** own either bank. The `defineQuizSection` selection requires explicit `questionIds` that may be owned by **different source Lessons**; the `defineFlashcardsSection` selection requires explicit `flashcardIds`. Section screen ContentBlocks never define Questions. A Quiz from external banks remains blocked for publication until #668's global UUID/locale resolver is merged and verified for the target textbook and locales. Build-only independent Question and Flashcard configurators produce separately addressable Props, and the host supplies a selected-resource reader to UI Kit. Complete-publication referential validation and independent YAML authoring stages are still pending.

`lesson.build.props({ languages })` produces `base` and sparse `locale` Props fragments. `lesson.build.overrides(patch, { languages })` prepares Course-specific patches without mutating the Lesson. Generators, not configurators, write files.

At runtime, [`assembleLessonProps`](../../../shared/engine/assemblers/assembleLessonProps/assembleLessonProps.ts) merges the published base and selected locale with optional Course overrides and validates the complete Props.

**Editorial localization:** for new Lessons, `lesson.yaml` uses `production.documentLanguage` for the source locale and `config.params.languages` for the intended Lesson language tuple. Historic `content.yaml` inputs require non-destructive migration. Remaining locales require separate translation, and interim TypeScript errors must stay visible until translations exist. Initial generation is not proof of publication readiness.

## YAML TypeScript draft authoring handoff

For new work, the Screenwriter enriches the single `lesson.yaml` technical/editorial source with Engine Config and separate authoring-only `production` nodes; legacy `content.yaml` is migration input, not a second active plan. The `production.documentLanguage` provenance is excluded from Runtime Config. The Engine YAML draft scaffolder converts it to incomplete named `*.data.ts` source using `@svitlo/engine/build` factories without validating, translating, typechecking or publishing. See [Config-shaped YAML handoff](../architecture/yaml-config-scaffolding.md). The Lesson Builder reads only `lesson.yaml`, excluding assessment banks; Course `config.yaml` stays unchanged. The published Props pipeline remains separate.

## Quest in standalone Lessons (planned runtime)

The [Quest Section](./quest.md) is designed for independently useful, accessible interactive demonstration and guided practice (e.g. manipulating a quadratic equation) without requiring characters or Course plot. It is not a Lesson subtype or a Quiz replacement. Quest Build contracts exist, but Lesson Section union, YAML and player integration are deferred. Do not publish a Quest as if supported; retain the approved Methodologist assessment and Skill boundaries.

### Independently authored Flashcards and Questions

The completed `defineFlashcard` Configurator retains typed flags, relationships and three structural sides; the front, back and optional hint use `defineFlashcardSide({ items, order })`. A side is _not_ independently identified and must not receive an invented UUID. The separate `flashcards/index.ts` TypeScript bank (when present) is compiled into individual UUID-addressed JSON objects, not a `Lesson.flashcards` collection. The Section's explicit `flashcardIds` determines order.

The separate Lesson-owned **Engine-shaped** YAML sources are `questions.yaml` and `flashcards.yaml`, with independent authoring, review and draft scaffolding stages. Independent Questions and Flashcards YAML-to-TypeScript draft builders now exist; use their actual Engine script README/tests, and keep source conversion distinct from Props publication. Refer to [the approved separation specification](../architecture/lesson-assessment-bank-separation.md) for the remaining work.

**Global UUID publication (PR #668 merged):** Authored Question and Flashcard bank sources remain Lesson-owned. The published data lives under `data/questions/<last-uuid-hex>/<uuid>/` and `data/flashcards/<last-uuid-hex>/<uuid>/` with a shared `data/assessments/manifest.json`. The manifest's `ownerLessonId` is provenance rather than a cross-Lesson Quiz selection restriction. Complete production validation and CI are required before treating this Draft migration as ready.

## Assessment Section decisions and Lesson completion gate

**Methodologist planning for independent and Course-born Lessons:** explicitly decide all four `LessonProductionAssessmentRequirements` production-only booleans: `questionBankRequired`, `flashcardBankRequired`, `quizSectionRequired`, `flashcardsSectionRequired`. Explain each choice and actual Question/Flashcard Bank ownership (including upstream owners for assessment-only Lessons) in the approved methodology. A bank and its in-Lesson Quiz/Flashcards Section are distinct; a bank may be needed without a Section. These booleans do **not** belong in `Lesson_Config` or runtime Props. Until the staged typed standalone Lesson flow exists, preserve the working `lesson.yaml` format and Section flags; record all four decisions in current Methodologist prose rather than introducing unknown YAML keys or a parallel tracking document. The future Course Stage 5 can create only an approved UUID-addressed `index.ts satisfies LessonWorkflowStage1`; no Lesson Configurator/Section and no Lesson PR. Only separately authorized later full Lesson materialization can emit pending `Not implemented` Sections, reserved IDs and `TODO (BLOCKER)` to prevent false-ready publication.

The root authoring-only `production.quizRequired` and `production.flashcardsRequired` flags optionally record Methodologist decisions about whether the **completed Lesson** eventually needs a Quiz or Flashcards Section. Absence or `false` means no such requirement, and no mandatory machine-readable inventory or methodology YAML fence is used. Multiple Sections can still be justified in ordinary prose and assigned unique IDs and ordered positions in `config.section.items/order`.
The Engine field `config.params.quizRequiredForCompletion` is separate from four Section/bank decisions: `true` requires a real **enabled Quiz Section** at completed Lesson build; `disabled: true` cannot fulfill it. It retains the authored value unchanged during materialization. Its current structural validation checks for an enabled Quiz Section when Props are built; the future runtime completion gate is a separate implementation responsibility.

The independent Question/Flashcard flows resolve real bank entries and approved Section selections. At initial Lesson materialization, incomplete assessment Sections **stay present, enabled and ordered** in generated TypeScript with fully typed mandatory Config parameters. Missing references get deterministic, reserved `feedc0de-` UUIDv8 values and explicit `TODO (BLOCKER)` comments, not fictitious bank entries or approvals. The full accepted Section definitions are also preserved in the temporary bank YAML `sectionDrafts` specialist handoff; the original `lesson.yaml` remains unchanged. A missing required Section gets one visibly provisional, deterministically identified minimal Section, not invented learner content. The distinct Engine `config.params.quizRequiredForCompletion` always preserves its authored value, including `true`; the draft can now satisfy structural validation without disabling the required-Quiz invariant. Production publisher and textbook resource/locale verification reject any unresolved reserved references even if comments are removed. Only independently authored, reviewed Question/Flashcard UUIDs and approved Quiz/Flashcards choices can replace placeholders. `/lesson finalize` may retire only source-equivalent, unchanged `lesson.yaml` after verified source transfer; it never approves or publishes a bank.
