# Assessment

**Status:** approved design for implementation in this PR; not a claim of existing runtime support.

An Assessment is a Course-owned ordered learning activity whose purpose is to elicit evidence of previously taught Skills. It is neither an instructional Lesson nor a narrative Episode. Course, Module and Chapter may each directly contain Assessments, Lessons and Episodes; Course may also contain Modules and Chapters, Module may contain Chapters. The entire Course may end with an Assessment without an artificial Module or Chapter. Instructional Lessons remain independently useful; replace the assessment-only Lesson exception for new authoring without silently deleting or migrating existing content.

## Ownership and contract

Use independent `defineAssessment`, `assessment_Config_Schema`, `assessment_Props_Schema` and inferred types, with immutable UUID, camelCase slug, `type: 'assessment'`, localized title, languages and ordered Sections. Reuse actual Section/ContentBlock configurators, Quiz selection, grading and global Question resolution. Require at least one enabled Quiz for completed Assessment; allow supporting Content Sections. No own Question Bank or Flashcard Bank, no Episode scenario/events, no Lesson wrapper, no duplicated Questions or new scoring engine. Flashcards remain learning/revision resources outside Assessment. The Assessment owns its selection and supporting content, not the source Questions. Use real imported Question Configurator IDs from reviewed Lesson banks, checking existence, supported locale and asset integrity. Do not invent IDs or silently select the entire bank.

## Course planning and dependencies

At Course Stage 1 require an explicit `production.assessment.required` boolean for Course-level assessment intent. At Stage 2 require the same explicit decision for every Module and Chapter as they are introduced. The decision applies to directly owned Assessment placements, not all descendants. Record rationale and planned coverage using existing methodology; do not duplicate Skills or other editorial fields unnecessarily. Stage 2 reserves ordered Assessment slots at the correct level; required means at least one direct slot, false means no direct slot. Stage 3 resolves dependencies against actual Lesson placements. Each Assessment has explicit production-only required Lesson dependencies, including additional Lessons when not covered by the owning scope. Use stable placement paths for not-yet-allocated Lessons and real UUID references for existing Lessons; resolve allocated identities during Course materialization. Reject unresolved references, ambiguous repeated placements and forward/cyclic dependencies within the Course. Distinguish educational/authoring readiness from runtime learner progression; do not invent a runtime dependsOn field. Before beginning Assessment production, verify required Lessons and their relevant reviewed Question Banks are ready and integrated into the Assessment branch ancestry; missing dependencies block Assessment production, not Course planning or draft preview.

## Two materialization levels

1. Course materialization allocates an immutable Assessment identity and creates a minimal valid inline Assessment placeholder in the generated owning Course/Module/Chapter configurator at the approved ordered slot. It must be visible in draft TOC/navigation and display “Not implemented” when opened, with a canonical `TODO (BLOCKER):` in authored source. Preserve approved title, placement, dependency handoff and locale plan without inventing Questions or creating a finished Assessment. Keep the placeholder structurally valid without casting or fake Question IDs. A distinct typed draft representation must not be mistaken for a completed Assessment.
2. Separately authorized Assessment materialization consumes its own reviewed typed editorial source, generates modular Assessment source with concrete Sections and ContentBlocks using existing renderers, replaces exactly the inline placeholder with the completed Configurator import/reference at the same UUID and order, and removes the consumed draft atomically. Resource specs, media and locale requirements remain real blockers until completed. Strict production rejects unresolved placeholders and resources; explicit draft preview supports incomplete Courses.

See the proposed [Assessment workflow](../../skills/assessment-workflow/create-assessment-flow.skill.md). Implement against the direct typed Zod-validated materialization architecture in PR #823; do not add another AST parser, custom resolver, redundant input alias or parallel YAML format.
