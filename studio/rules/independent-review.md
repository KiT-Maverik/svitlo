# Independent review

An independent review is performed in a new chat. Follow the applicable entity workflow, inspect the actual authored source, correct in-scope defects and record the final verdict and reviewed revision in the existing entity PR.

For Course, Episode, Lesson, Question Bank and Flashcard Bank, reviews, stage checklists and methodology decisions are editorial responsibilities. Record actual findings, corrections, verdicts and the commit/blob SHAs actually examined in human-readable form in the existing entity PR. Earlier completed stages retain their historical reviewed revisions; later corrective stages record their own final source revision and verdict. Never invent approvals or imply that an earlier review examined newer bytes.

These records are not machine-readable prerequisites for materialization. All five trusted commands require explicit authorized invocation and valid pinned typed source; they never parse PR descriptions, checklist status, editorial markers or review verdicts. Technical identity, source integrity, safe Git transactions and publication checks remain independent. An editorial verdict is not authorization to invoke a command, publish or merge.
