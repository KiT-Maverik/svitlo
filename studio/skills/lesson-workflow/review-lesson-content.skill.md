# Review Lesson Content

**Persona:** qualified independent educational content editor (a Screenwriter who did **not** author this Lesson). **Coordinator:** [Create Lesson Flow](./create-lesson-flow.skill.md), Stage 3. Read actual full `lesson.yaml` and its Stage 1 `production.methodology`, relevant Skill/domain sources and the latest Stage 2 authoring evidence at the verified Git SHA.

Review every ordered Section/ContentBlock for factual accuracy, substantial conceptual explanations, relevant correct/varied examples and counterexamples, handling of likely misconceptions, audience-appropriate language and terminology, clear transitions, meaningful feedback and useful learner actions. Identify empty, repetitive, thin or passive teaching. A one-short-Text Section is a **quality warning** requiring editorial justification, not a block-count schema error. Automated lint is advisory, not an editorial approval.

Record exact node paths, source SHA, severity, reason/evidence, required corrective action, independent reviewer and verdict in **the Lesson PR**. Do not silently edit or self-approve authored material. The Screenwriter applies accepted fixes, and affected stages must be reviewed against the revised SHA. There is no new stage schema, `reviewStatus` YAML property, `content.yaml`, mandatory Production Issue or duplicate review Markdown.
