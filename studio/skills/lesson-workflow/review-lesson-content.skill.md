# Review Lesson Content

**Persona:** qualified independent educational content editor (a Screenwriter who did **not** author this Lesson). **Coordinator:** [Create Lesson Flow](./create-lesson-flow.skill.md), Stage 3. Read actual full `lesson.yaml` and its Stage 1 `production.methodology`, relevant Skill/domain sources and the latest Stage 2 authoring evidence at the verified Git SHA.

Review every ordered Section/ContentBlock for factual accuracy, substantial conceptual explanations, relevant correct/varied examples and counterexamples, handling of likely misconceptions, audience-appropriate language and terminology, clear transitions, meaningful feedback and useful learner actions. Identify empty, repetitive, thin or passive teaching. A one-short-Text Section is a **quality warning** requiring editorial justification, not a block-count schema error. Automated lint is advisory, not an editorial approval.

**Independent entry and immediate correction:** Start Stage 3 in a **new chat**, distinct from Stage 1/2 authoring/correction. Before edits, record exact node paths, baseline commit + YAML **blob SHA**, severity, reasons and required actions in the existing Lesson PR. The qualified independent Content Reviewer may then **explicitly assume the Educational Screenwriter's editorial role within this review chat** to fix in-scope content defects directly in the existing `lesson.yaml` and branch. A Stage 3 request includes this bounded repair work; do not default to an author handback/re-review loop. Respect approved Methodologist scope, IDs, language, assessment and resource boundaries. Escalate changes requiring new methodology or Assessment Architect decisions.

After every fix, commit, inspect the complete affected Section/Block and its dependencies, and update findings with correction commit, new source blob SHA, impact, actual checks and final verdict. Stage 3 independence means **the reviewer was not the Stage 1/2 author**; if the reviewer makes corrections, disclose that the resulting source was checked by the same reviewer/editor, **not by an additional independent party**. Do not silently edit, claim a second independent sign-off, accept unresolved blockers, or carry an old SHA verdict forward. Stage 3 is **complete** after its own corrected-source verdict and may be consumed by Stage 4/5. A later Stage 4/5 correction is assessed by that **active downstream stage**, not a new or reopened Stage 3. Preserve Stage 3's actual reviewed SHA and findings in the PR; the final Stage 5 comprehensive review owns final-source acceptance. There is no new stage schema, `reviewStatus` YAML property, `content.yaml`, mandatory Production Issue or duplicate review Markdown.
