## Additional experience and assessment boundaries

2. **Current ContentBlock names:** `text`, `code`, `image`, `video`, `youtube`, `carousel`, `scene`, `checkpoint`, `markdown`, `audio`, `subtitles` (the Engine `contentBlockTypes` union). The `subtitles` ContentBlock authors a localized WebVTT file reference; UI Kit renders the resolved `fileLocation` as a standalone keyboard-accessible resource link, not a synchronized video caption track. Supply the actual source-language captions or their production specification; flag synchronized playback as a separate runtime dependency. Do not use the obsolete `blockType` property or invent new supported types. For each block, record its editorial `key`, actual type, pedagogical purpose, source copy/structured spec, implementation `readiness`, and accessible alternative. Read current renderer and typed contracts to assess feasibility.

**Composite placement:** the Lesson's ordinary Section content may contain both Primitive (`text`, `markdown`, `code`, `image`, `audio`, `video`, `youtube`, `subtitles`, `scene`) and Composite (`carousel`, `checkpoint`) blocks. A Carousel slide may be Image/Scene only; a CheckpointItem has a Text question and a CheckpointAnswer with **Primitive-only** blocks. Do not place Carousel/Checkpoint inside a CheckpointAnswer; a direct Scene answer is valid. Preserve the original `items/order`, UUID, locale and reviewed learning intent. See [composition matrix](../../docs/architecture/content-block-composition.md).

3. **Question ownership:** separately owned assessment briefs may specify intended evidence and cognitive operation, but must not invent the actual Question bank, correct responses, scoring, grading or mastery policy. The Assessment Architect produces validated items. Do not assume each cognitive operation corresponds to one Question type; the type is a UI interaction mechanism.

4. **Experience quality:** use prediction, visualization, exploration, useful feedback, worked examples, retrieval and transfer as appropriate. No compulsory theory/practice/quiz recipe; no decorative interaction quotas. Preserve accuracy, cognitive load, age suitability, inclusive keyboard/touch access, RTL, low-end/offline equivalence and meaningful non-visual alternatives. A `checkpoint` is a learning/reflection block, **not** scored Quiz evidence.

## Committed instructional media and separate production blockers

For **instructional Lesson Section/ContentBlock content only**, treat each selected, fully specified image, audio, video or Carousel medium as present in the **designed** final experience, regardless of whether its physical asset has been produced. Write the actual source-language explanation, learner observation, action and feedback about what that media depicts or says. **Never** put “if the image/video is available”, “if generated”, “if licensed” or “otherwise use another asset” into learner-facing content merely because production is incomplete. Accessible equivalent paths and genuine learner choices remain required, but do not depend on whether a planned file currently exists.

Make the resource brief implementable: document exact visual/audio content and relationships in `production.specification` and `production.prompt` (where relevant); add source-language script/captions/transcript, `production.accessibility` and `production.offline`. For missing or unverified resources, use `production.readiness: 'production-pending'` and a specific `TODO (BLOCKER):` in the authoring-only specification or Lesson PR recording what the producer must deliver/check. Do **not** invent absent `locale.fileName`, `posterFileName` or YouTube `href`. The Stage 6.1 materializer inserts its canonical reserved reference/blocker, and Stage 6 must supply and verify the asset before publication.

For externally sourced media, verify licenses/permission for direct use, adaptation, attribution, distribution and offline packaging. If reuse rights are unclear, do not place the third-party file in the Lesson. When legally permitted to use the source as a reference, direct production of a **genuinely original** AI-generated or separately composed medium based on factual concepts and lesson needs, not a close copy of protected expression; do not upload a third-party reference to a generator without appropriate rights. Record original reference/provenance, rights-review dependencies and a specific generation/verification blocker in `production`/the PR. Copyright uncertainty must not create conditional learner copy.

## Quest Section plans (Build contract exists; runtime pending)

A Quest is neither an ordinary ordered `blocks` Section nor a Quiz; see [Quest](../../docs/entities/quest.md). When the approved learning experience requires an isolated, stateful interactive workspace, the editorial Lesson `draft.ts` Stage 2 source may describe `type: quest` with named Stage and Task proposals, including mandatory `start`, exactly one Scene specification for each Task, all planned media/caption/accessibility production requirements and explicit branches determined by Scene actions. Never invent a `blocks` list or positional `order` inside Quest; record story-free learning flows in neutral Lessons and story missions in Episodes. The typed Stage 2 contract supports Quest, and the source materializer emits Build-only Quest/Stage/Task modules with a `TODO (BLOCKER)` diagnostic for missing runtime integration. The Lesson/Episode runtime Section union and UI do not support Quest playback or publication. Maintain a specific nested `production` specification of the pending runtime dependency until implemented and independently validated.

## Source-content and implementation-quality requirements

The Stage 2 authored typed Config-shaped node is **learner-facing material**, not a list of instructions for later agents to invent the material. For Text and Markdown, provide the real explanatory copy, questions and feedback in source-language `locale` fields. For Code, provide the actual source in the current `params.codeAsText` contract. Markdown must contain full examples, inline and displayed math where appropriate, with accessible verbal alternatives and correct literal-dollar handling; consult [Markdown ContentBlock](../../docs/entities/markdown-content-block.md). The integrated typed materializer later extracts authored Markdown/Code into adjacent files. An empty block with `production.specification` alone is **not** a complete teaching block.

- **Structure and sequence:** organize coherent Sections into explicit ordered collections, identify their educational purposes, and provide meaningful teaching transitions. Avoid one-topic-per-UI-element padding; a single well-developed explanation can be better than artificial interaction.
- **Conceptual learning:** for each target outcome, provide grounded definitions, worked examples and counterexamples, error/misconception diagnosis, practice or thought exercises when useful, and accurate feedback with opportunities to adjust understanding. Different learner actions should serve different cognitive goals rather than mechanically repeating a pattern.
- **Assessment boundaries:** actual Quiz remains a Section, not a ContentBlock. Honor all Methodologist Quiz intents/target Skills/Assessment Methods. A checkpoint or exploratory Scene is formative interaction, not a substitute for the independent full Question Bank or scoring policy. Distinguish reviewed Question intentions from implemented Question IDs.
- **Media and Scenes:** use verified local media IDs when available, otherwise omit unavailable `locale.fileName` (including Video `posterFileName`), or unavailable YouTube `locale.href` while retaining required learner-facing copy and complete production specifications. Stage 6.1 inserts the reserved blocked reference; never invent a YouTube URL; write production specs precise enough for a separate specialist to implement without guessing. State learner purpose, inputs/actions, transitions, expected feedback, numerical/content invariants, failure/recovery paths, text equivalents, captions/transcripts and device/accessibility constraints. Never bake unique user-facing copy into nonlocalizable image pixels without accessible text.
- **Technology honesty:** inspect implemented UI Kit/renderer before promising Scene/Quest interaction, graded behavior, offline availability or animations. Planned behavior can be reviewed at Stages 3–5 but must not be presented as shipped.
- **Source provenance:** maintain stable UUIDs/slugs and only actual source-locale copy; preserve exact teaching and source revisions through authorized edits. Preserve the approved runtime completion policy independently of the four editorial assessment decisions.

This content quality checklist informs editorial reviews; schema validity at Stage 6 remains a separate technical check.

**Typed Stage 2 completion:** retain the **same** Stage 1 export, enrich `config.section.items/order` with plain nested `{ config, production? }` nodes and use `satisfies LessonWorkflowStage2`. Do not create `define*` configurators at this editorial stage. Scenes/media require implementable `production.specification`, accessibility and offline equivalents; Quest is explicitly `runtimeStatus: 'unimplemented'` until real renderer support is proven. The Section/Block materializer, not the Screenwriter, later emits code. Stage 3 reviews this same object in a new context.
